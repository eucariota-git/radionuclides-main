        Method — formulas, sources and adaptations

        For each nuclide, with A the activity reaching the sewer in one year and DF = exp(−λ·ttransit):

        Scenario 1 (NUREG/CR-5814 §5.2.1, transit ≈ 0.2 h, dilution in the facility's annual water volume V):

        • Cwater [Bq/m³] = A · DF / V; Cair [Bq/m³] = (Cwater / ρwater) · aerosol loading · fresp
        • Eext = Cwater · k1 · text · g; Einh = Cair · BR · tinh · e(inh)

        Scenario 2 (NUREG/CR-5814 §5.2.2, transit ≈ 3 d, the year's discharge retained in the year's sludge):

        • Cdry [Bq/kg] = A · fsludge · DF / (Mwet · fsolids); Cwet [Bq/m³] = A · fsludge · DF · ρwet / Mwet
        • Cair [Bq/m³] = Cdry · dust loading · fresp
        • Eext = Cwet · k2 · text · fgeom; Einh = Cair · BR · tinh · e(inh)

        No decay during the exposure year is applied. The concentration is the annual discharge divided by the annual water volume (Scenario 1) or by the annual sludge production (Scenario 2), with transit decay only; this is a screening assumption — a continuous discharge does not by itself demonstrate mixing or steady state. fsludge is the retained fraction as entered (default: 100%). k1 and k2 are effective-dose-rate coefficients (ICRP Publication 116 (2010), ROT) calculated with PHITS by José Ramón Sendón del Río for the geometry of each scenario — they are not point-source Γ constants and only apply to that geometry.

        Doses are summed per nuclide within each scenario; the two scenarios concern different receptors and are never added together. Not a complete discharge assessment: the IS-28 Annex II II.A.4 sewer conditions (per-nuclide concentration level, sum of fractions, annual activity limits) and mixture clearance are evaluated separately.

        Inhalation coefficients are those for adult members of the public (RD 783/2001 Annex III table b), kept by the CSN Resolution of 3 April 2024; transcription: ICRP Publication 119 (2012), Table G.1), lung absorption type F (fast) or M (moderate). They apply to an inhaled particulate aerosol of AMAD 1 µm (ICRP 119), not to gases or vapours (e.g. elemental iodine vapour). Half-lives are taken from the app database (Lu-177m, not a curated entry, from ICRP 107).
