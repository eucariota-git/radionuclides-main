Caregiver Dose — External Exposure after Discharge

External dose to one caregiver, family member or third party from a radionuclide-therapy patient (Lu-177, I-131), from the dose rate measured at 1 m at discharge, a two-phase effective kinetics, the daily cohabitation pattern during and after a restriction period, and equal treatment cycles. Inverse calculations give the maximum discharge rate and the minimum restriction that meet the selected criterion.
Screening estimate for one person and equal cycles. The integral of the measured operational quantity is used as an estimator of external exposure; it is not automatically the individual effective dose nor the fetal dose. Internal contamination, the dose before discharge and other sources are not included. The criteria per category are evaluation criteria adopted by the department, not values fixed by regulation for this treatment.

🏠 B. Cohabitation

Hours are mutually exclusive: sleeping is not counted as day-time cohabitation. «Sleeping in the same room» during the restriction is usually 0 h (sleeping apart). Zero hours = no contact; the distance may then be left empty. The distance is chosen from the profile list; 0.1 m = contact. Days are counted from discharge and each cycle is integrated to infinity. The criterion covers only the administrations entered; it does not by itself demonstrate annual compliance.

    📖 Method

    With Ḣ0 the rate at 1 m at discharge, tr = max(0, tfast end − tdischarge) the remaining duration of the fast phase, λ = ln 2 / Teff (λf = λs when tr = 0), and two consecutive exponentials (the slow phase starts from the rate reached at the end of the fast phase):

    • Itail = Ḣ0 e−λftr / λs; I∞ = Ḣ0 (1 − e−λftr) / λf + Itail
    • For a restriction of C days: Ibefore(C) = ∫0C Ḣ dt and Iafter(C) = ∫C∞ Ḣ dt
    • Dose per cycle and row = Ibefore · hduring/24 · fduring + Iafter · hafter/24 · fafter, with f the spatial factor of the profile relative to 1 m; treatment dose = cycles × Σ rows
    • fr = Σ hduring fduring / 24, fn = Σ hafter fafter / 24; floor = cycles · fr · I∞
    • Maximum rate = Ḣ0 · criterion / treatment dose (the dose is proportional to the rate). Minimum restriction: the C that solves cycles · [fr I∞ + (fn − fr) Iafter(C)] = criterion, analytically in either phase

    Integration in SI (Sv/s, s, Sv); the interface uses µSv/h, h, d and mSv.
