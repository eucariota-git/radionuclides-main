Reference & data notes — sources, method and limits of the values above
  • 32 nuclides: Cornejo et al. (2015) provides published comparison values, and its air-kerma constants are the ones shown in the K-air column. H*(10) and H'(0.07) are recalculated using the stated nuclear data (ICRP 107, 2008) and conversion coefficients (ICRP 74, 1996, Table A.21), except the H*(10) of Cs-137 and Mo-99+Tc-99m, which keep the published value; 24 of the 32 H*(10) values lie within ±2 % of the published ones, those two included. Where they differ, the difference has not been reconciled.
  • Ga-68, Zr-89, Cu-67, Tb-161, Ho-166, Ra-223 and Ac-225: no published comparison; calculated in the same way. For the two alpha-therapy chains (Ra-223, Ac-225) the constants assume all progeny retained in the source and in secular equilibrium — check it for the actual source (preparation time, loss of gaseous progeny).
  • Y-90: predominantly a β⁻ emitter with no photon line passing the filter; photon dose-rate constants are not applicable on this page. Dose offers a separate, explicitly experimental bremsstrahlung scenario with hypothetical container values; direct beta dose is not calculated.
  • H*(10) and H'(0.07) are both AREA-monitoring operational quantities and are always shown side by side: H*(10) estimates effective dose, H'(0.07) indicates skin/extremity dose. Neither bounds the other (H*(10) is not always the higher figure — for Pd-103 the stored ΓH*(10) is about 40% below ΓH'(0.07)). Personal dosimetry quantities are Hp(10) / Hp(0.07) / Hp(3); limit comparisons in the dose page are indicative screening only.
  • Clearance — Table A1 (RD 1217/2024 Annex IV, 257 entries): activity concentration for any quantity of solid material; the same values give the exemption of unlimited quantities (Annex II A.1.c). This is the value of the «Exemption / clearance» column. Not tabulated: Annex III.1.b refers to the European Commission's "Radiation Protection 122 Part 1".
  • Exemption of moderate quantities — Table B (≤ 1000 kg): two separate criteria, not simultaneous conditions — column 3, total activity in Bq, for radioactive sources (Annex II A.1.a), and column 2, activity concentration in kBq/kg, for materials in moderate quantity (A.1.b). Shown in the detail panel. Not tabulated: Annex II A.3.a (values set by the CSN; the app includes those of the CSN Resolution of 16 June 2025, Y-88 and Ba-133, shown as «CSN value»).
  • Both are individual values for one nuclide (mixtures are not evaluated); Decay uses them for single-nuclide estimates.
  • Liquid effluent column (individual discharge concentration level, every nuclide): (1 mSv / e(g)) / 600 L, IS-28 Annex II II.A.4, with the adult ingestion coefficient e(g) of RD 783/2001 Annex III table a), kept in force by RD 1029/2022 and the CSN Resolution of 3 April 2024 (numerical transcription: ICRP 119 Table F.1); the most restrictive chemical form governs. It is only one condition of II.A.4: the mixture sum of fractions ≤ 1 and the annual activity limits (10 GBq H-3, 1 GBq C-14, < 1 GBq the rest combined) are not evaluated here, and II.A.4 also requires an express discharge authorisation and soluble or readily dispersible material. The facility's authorisation prevails.
  • Example applications shown for the curated nuclides are illustrative. They mix current, historical and research uses and do not indicate marketing authorisation, clinical status or availability in Spain.
