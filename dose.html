    Shielding details

    Shielding methods available The method used is named above: the Archer broad-beam fit (Oumano et al. 2025, full photon spectrum) only where a fit exists for the nuclide and material — Tc-99m, F-18, I-131 and Lu-177 behind lead or concrete; otherwise spectrum-weighted narrow-beam attenuation with build-up (dose-weighted sum over the ICRP 107 (2008) photon lines with point-isotropic exposure build-up factors, ANSI/ANS-6.4.3; infinite-medium values, see NUREG/CR-5740 for finite-slab corrections that can move the result in either direction). Barrier scatter is approximated by the selected transmission model.
