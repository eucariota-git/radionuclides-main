# Effluent — invariants

Effluent page (`effluent.html`): Scenarios 1 (sewer inspector) and 2 (STP sludge operator) are DIFFERENT receptors — never add their doses. Source term: A_adm = patients × cycles × administered activity (all excreted); the in-hospital fraction leaves through the facility (for nuclides in decay tanks only their proportional share of the annual tank discharge, default 1 GBq/y for the sum, no decay credit); the rest is excreted at home. Scenario 1 gets only what leaves the hospital, diluted in the facility's annual water volume; Scenario 2 gets that plus the home part × catchment fraction; its PHITS k (600 × 200 × 50 cm water slab, receptor at 1 m) is photon-only, bremsstrahlung not bounded. A nuclide without a Scenario 2 external coefficient (F-18) keeps its inhalation; its external pathway is null (never zero) and the scenario total is flagged as a PARTIAL sum (`complete: false`, `missing`) everywhere. Solids fraction, 5090 t/y and the 1 GBq/y tank default are site hypotheses / boundary values, not regulatory values. Scenario 2: screening model, all discharged activity retained in one year of sludge (C = Q/S, SRS-19 §4.9 b), decay only during transit to the press. External coefficients k are own PHITS effective-dose-rate values (ICRP 116 ROT) for 10 × 10 × 3 m and 45 × 45 × 3 m sludge masses, receptor at 2 m — never replace them with point-source Γ, and never claim they cover the infinite slab NUREG prescribes (Lu-177 L45 captures 73–75 % of it). Inhalation uses ICRP 119 G.1 adult PUBLIC coefficients (Lu-177/Lu-177m/Tc-99m/F-18 type M, I-131 type F), for an inhaled particulate aerosol of AMAD 1 µm — not for gases or vapours. I-131 has NO default administered activity and is not included by default (user decision 2026-10-30: the workbook's 0.8 GBq is the activity at discharge, so it misrepresented the source term) — never reinstate a default; the field may note, as information, that patients usually leave hospital with less than 0.8 GBq. The dose criterion is user-defined; do not attribute a regulatory basis to it. Catchment default 100 % (all patients in the STP catchment, conservative; user decision 2026-11-01, was the workbook's 40 %). Distributed texts (data, page, report, guides) never cite the reference workbooks, their sheets or cells, or the PRTR plant code: a default cites its primary source or is stated as an illustrative, site-specific value (Test 36). The workbook's I-131 shortcut (NUREG B.7 factor rescaled by dry mass only) is replaced by the explicit model and kept only as an indicative cross-check.
