# Caregiver — invariants

Caregiver inverse: with more weighted contact during the restriction than after it (fr > fn) restricting INCREASES the dose — the answer is 0 d if the 0-day dose meets the criterion, else «restriction increases», and the indefinite-restriction value is an upper asymptote, not a floor (deliberate deviation from the workbook; the usual case fr < fn still reproduces the workbook and the 324 cells of Gayol Vilorio et al. 2025, whose 6 h tables round to the nearest day although the text says «por defecto»).

Caregiver page (`caregiver.html`): faithful port of the workbook v2.1 (sheets Dosis, Curva, Comprobación) — keep it numerically identical to the workbook (test reproduces its preloaded case and curve to 1e-9; that case = page defaults + 3 d, `wbInput()` in the tests). Without days only the schedule AFTER the restriction is checked against 24 h/d (a 0-day answer never applies «during»; audit A-03), and the self-check checks every inverse branch and never reports OK with nothing checked (A-02). Since v1.17.1 the restriction days start EMPTY (the workbook's 3 d looked like a recommendation): the inverse results, the curve and the self-check work without them (`out.tableReady`); table dose, margin and maximum rate need them (`out.ready`). Do not default the days to an SmPC or Forum number. Occupancy is the daily average h/24 (as the workbook, Forum and Gayol); `occupancyBounds` only bounds it for any timetable with the declared hours in every 24 h interval from discharge (none when a contact-changing restriction ends inside a day — a split day has no defined hours) — informative, never applied to the dose or the inverse, no criterion attached. Two CONSECUTIVE exponentials (the slow phase starts from the rate reached at the end of the fast phase; never a simultaneous sum of components), each cycle integrated to infinity. The measured operational quantity is used as an estimator of external exposure, not as effective or fetal dose. Distances come only from the profile list (no interpolation); contact 0.1 m takes the 0.4 m factor (1/0.4)² in the approximate profiles. The criteria per category (3/5/1/0.3 mSv) are evaluation criteria adopted in this application (never «department» or «servicio»: texts must suit any professional), not regulatory values for this treatment (RD 601/2019 requires carer constraints but sets no number): 3 mSv ≤ 60 y and 0.3 mSv third persons from EC RP 97 / Foro 2011; 5 mSv > 60 y from ICRP 103 para. 351 / ICRP 94 / IAEA SSG-46 para. 2.49 (international 5 mSv per episode), applied to the whole treatment. Do not add the 15 mSv of RP 97 just because it is published. The criterion never changes the calculated dose.

Caregiver cohabitation scenarios (`cohabitation_scenarios`, v1.17.0): Foro 2011 Table 3.B 1 + its co-worker paragraph, as printed. They fill hours/distances of both rows and propose the category; never the restriction days; a category other than the one adopted in this application for the scenario is a warning, not a block (children: public criterion at every age — the app's decision, not the Forum's); any manual edit → free scenario (user names the rows). With Table 3.B 1 the app reproduces Tables 3.B 3/3.B 4 for partner < 60 (2 mSv), children < 1, 2–5, 5–11 (1 mSv) and general work (0.3 mSv); NOT > 11 y (2 cells), 1–2 y (8) and work with children (1) — the tables cite «Rivas 2002»; do not «fix» Table 3.B 1 by fitting (too many free parameters). Children of any age: public criterion (1 mSv) — the child scenarios propose and accept only `public` (user decision 2026-10-22; Forum Table 2's 3 mSv over 10 years is not used). Preset `spread`: only the statistic the source publishes (Kurth 2018 slow T½ SD = 15.8 h from Table 3; the abstract's 9.6 h is the 72 h column), never an SD derived from ranges or quartiles.
